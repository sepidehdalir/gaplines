GapLines privacy policy · Updated October 18, 2026

Inside the app

GapLines records only when you choose Record and grant microphone permission. Audio recordings, speaker labels, scene titles, reply-gap timing and gap presets are stored in the app on your device. We do not receive or upload this content. The app has no account, advertising, tracking SDK, AI voice service, transcription service or cloud backend.

Recordings stop when the app leaves the foreground. Local aggregate counters help track app actions on your device; they contain no dialogue, labels, user or device identifiers and are not sent to us.

Sharing and recovery

You can choose to export WAV audio or a portable scene backup. These files can contain private recordings and labels. The destination you select in Files or the share sheet determines who can access them; its service policy applies. Ordinary device backups may include app data according to your system settings. GapLines does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

Delete a scene in the app to remove it from your visible library. Previous immutable takes may remain in the app for recovery protection. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to your device backup settings. Copies you shared or saved elsewhere remain under the destination's control.

Purchases and external services

Apple handles in-app purchase processing. GapLines checks signed StoreKit entitlements and does not receive your payment credentials or send receipts to a developer server. Apple's policies apply to its services. Opening this public website may expose normal web access information to its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, under that provider's policy; this website has no added analytics or tracking scripts.

Support

If you choose to email support, we receive the information you send to respond to you. Please do not include private recordings, passwords or payment details. Contact hello@arvinify.com.
